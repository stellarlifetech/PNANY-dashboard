# PNANY Dashboard: Driven by Purpose, United in Mission

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24217696.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24217696)

An interactive, single-page dashboard about the leadership network of the **Philippine Nurses Association of New York (PNANY)**, 2026–2028.

**Live dashboard:** https://stellarlifetech.github.io/PNANY-dashboard/

**DOI (all versions):** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24217696

## What is inside

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Welcome screen** | A "Click to begin your ride!" page with the PNANY jeepney, the subtitle "More mileage, bigger goals, broader horizons.", a faint waving U.S. flag backdrop, and an optional jeepney horn (only the horn plays there; dashboard sound starts after you click to begin). |
| **Leader network** | A 3D multilayer network of the 17 leaders across five layers: team members, where they work, shout-outs on LinkedIn, past PNANY service, and mission work. It rotates and can be dragged, and each layer can be peeled off. |
| **Find help** | A "Who can help me with…" finder and leader profiles that link only to public, professional pages. |
| **World goals** | A map of PNANY's work to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, with evidence ratings. |
| **New members** | A Monte Carlo and Bayesian simulator (5,000,000 runs) of how new members could link in. |
| **Statistical models** | Nine interactive models: path model, system dynamics, agent-based, regression (with bootstrap), Bayesian (with a live sampler), Monte Carlo, resilience stress test, crisis surge, and event carbon footprint. Each has insights and an "About these results" note. |
| **Key numbers** | Network measures, a ranked table of leaders, and a list of public sources. |
| **Listen** | An orchestral sonification of the network, a harmony meter, and an interactive "open chair" invitation. |
| **Human Factors Lab** | A biocomputing-inspired simulation: the 17 leaders act as a "brain on a chip" that learns to drive a jeepney through New York traffic and park it, and shows a mood, a sense of control and teamwork, and changes the lighting. |
| **References** | 78 published sources, numbered and linked from the text. |

## Data and privacy

- Network links come only from **public, professional sources**: the PNANY officers flyer, LinkedIn, workplace and school pages, award and association pages, news stories, and public résumés.
- **Personal social media accounts are left out on purpose**, and no personal phone numbers or emails of the leaders are shown.
- Leaders who want something added, changed, or removed can use the contact details at the bottom of the dashboard.

## About the simulations

The models use accepted methods from published research, but their inputs are public information plus stated assumptions. **They are simulations, not measurements, and have not been checked against PNANY's own records.** Use them to explore ideas and plan, not to judge individual people or to make final decisions. Each model explains its own limits on the page.
